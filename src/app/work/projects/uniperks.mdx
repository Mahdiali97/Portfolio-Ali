## Overview

A full-stack mobile commerce ecosystem engineered specifically for Universiti Pendidikan Sultan Idris (UPSI) students and faculty. The platform transforms standard university merchandise transactions into an engaging retention loop by pairing direct commerce with a daily educational quiz and coin-reward system.

## Core Architecture & Features

- **Gamified Economy**: A real-time coin-generation engine tied to timed academic quizzes, allowing students to earn discounts through daily engagement.
- **Voucher & Wallet Subsystem**: Secure digital wallet managing coupon redemption logic, expiration rules, and automated checkout discounts.
- **Role-Based Management**: Distinct dashboards allowing administrators to manage inventory and verify student claims while students browse and checkout.

## Technical Stack & Tools

- **Mobile App**: Flutter, Dart (Cross-Platform iOS & Android)
- **Backend & Database**: Supabase (PostgreSQL, Realtime Subscriptions, Row-Level Security)
- **Authentication & Security**: Firebase Auth, Supabase Auth
- **Payments**: Stripe API Integration
- **Design & Prototyping**: Figma
