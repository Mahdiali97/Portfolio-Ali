01

Overview

GEP CRM System (GuardExpert Pro) is a specialized customer and operations management platform designed for security service providers. It centralizes company subscriptions, zone management, premise tracking, and automated invoicing into a single, intuitive dashboard.

The goal was to design a clean, responsive web interface that eliminates manual spreadsheet errors and provides real-time visibility into active security contracts, client locations, and monthly billing.

  • End-to-end UI/UX design in Figma
  • Responsive web framework implementation
  • Secure authentication & role-based access
  • Relational database architecture

Key Modules

Company & Zone Mgmt

Register client companies, assign geographical security zones, and manage contract terms.

Premise Tracking

Log and monitor individual security premises, assign guards, and track active coverage.

Subscription & Invoicing

Automate monthly billing, track paid vs. pending accounts, and export invoice logs.

Operations Reporting

Real-time executive dashboard showing revenue metrics, active subscriptions, and zone coverage.

02

Existing Design & Problem Statement

Before GEP CRM, security management companies relied heavily on disjointed spreadsheets and manual record-keeping to track client premises and generate invoices. A workflow audit revealed four critical failure points:

  • Fragmented client data scattered across multiple legacy files
  • Manual security zone and premise assignment was error-prone and slow
  • Delayed monthly invoicing leading to cash flow friction
  • No centralized executive dashboard to view active subscriptions and revenue metrics at a glance

03

Competitive Analysis

I analyzed existing enterprise CRM solutions and field-service management platforms to identify best practices in data density, table navigation, and status tracking.

Standard Enterprise CRMs

Great for pipeline management, but lack specialized spatial features like multi-zone security mapping and physical premise tracking. Overly generic for niche security operations.

Field Service Tools

Excellent tracking mechanics, but cluttered user interfaces that require extensive staff training. Navigation patterns are deeply nested and counter-intuitive for administrative staff.

GEP Approach

Combined the clean, structured data-tables of modern CRMs with a retro-styled, highly intuitive operator dashboard — purpose-built for security companies.

04

User Research

I conducted interviews and workflow walk-throughs with operations managers and administrative staff. Key findings that shaped the entire information architecture:

  • 85% of managers preferred tabular data displays with quick-filter options over deep nested menus
  • Real-time tracking of active vs. terminated subscriptions was cited as the highest priority metric
  • Automated invoice calculation saved an estimated 4+ hours of administrative work per week
